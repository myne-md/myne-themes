# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want `myne-themes` to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone who
shares or uses a theme, regardless of experience, identity, or background.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind and respectful in issues, reviews, and PRs.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on a theme.
- Crediting others' work and respecting licenses.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language — including in theme names,
  descriptions, or `meta.json` fields.
- Submitting deceptive or malicious themes (see [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md)).
- Publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers (see [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS)) may edit, reject, or
remove contributions, comments, and themes that violate this Code of Conduct, and
may block repeat offenders from contributing.

Report concerns by opening a confidential issue or contacting a maintainer.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
